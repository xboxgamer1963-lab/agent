How It Works

Four steps to enriched pipeline

1

Connect your Google Sheet

Paste your sheet ID, enter your value proposition, and click Start. That's your entire configuration. No ETL, no CSV uploads.

2

Agents fan out in parallel

Five accounts process concurrently. Eight agents per account cover web research, news, LinkedIn, scoring, and dossier writing simultaneously.

3

Strict scoring with auto-retry

Accounts below your threshold are re-evaluated with a different hypothesis angle. Still below? Dropped. Above? Shipped to Pipedrive.

check

Qualified leads hit your CRM

Ledger creates the Pipedrive org, attaches the full dossier, applies custom labels, and adds LinkedIn Boolean search strings. Your reps show up prepared.
